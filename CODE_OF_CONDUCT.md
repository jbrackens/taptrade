# Code Of Conduct

Tap Trade contributors are expected to keep collaboration direct, kind, and useful.

## Standards

- Be respectful when discussing bugs, tradeoffs, and review feedback.
- Assume good intent, but be precise about risks and evidence.
- Keep feedback about code, docs, product behavior, or process rather than about people.
- Make room for questions from people who are new to prediction markets, exchange systems, or this codebase.
- Report security-sensitive issues privately through the security process instead of opening a public issue.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic disruption in issues, pull requests, or docs discussions.
- Dismissing safety, compliance, or financial-risk concerns without investigation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block contributors who repeatedly violate these expectations.

For private reports, use the contact path in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
