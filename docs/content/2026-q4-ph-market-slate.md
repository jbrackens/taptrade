## Legal / sensitivity caveats

- All pageant questions are scoped to **placement/winning only** — no questions about contestants' bodies, relationships, or personal lives, per the brief's own guidance.
- Politics-economy desk is scoped to **procedural/data events only** (rate decisions, FX levels, inflation prints, a budget-signing deadline). Explicitly avoided any market touching the ongoing Senate impeachment-trial coverage that surfaced during research, or any other personality-driven political storyline — that falls under "personal scandals" territory the brief asked to exclude.
- No market in this slate touches deaths, injuries, disasters, arrests, health conditions, or minors.
- No "bet/odds/wager/cash/win money/prize" language appears in any question; all are sentence case, end in "?", and are under ~110 characters.
- Every "void" clause is written so a postponed/cancelled event refunds points rather than forcing a resolution on an event that didn't happen as described — flag this pattern for whoever builds the actual resolution engine.
